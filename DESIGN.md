## Overview

Extend the operator-approved visual language already implemented in
admin-api/dashboard/index.html. Tonal panels and measured data carry the
interface; primary actions are easy to locate without decorative effects.

The customer sales page (`/portal/<locale>/`, server-rendered by
`admin-api/portal_landing.go`) is the one marketing surface. It may be more
expressive (wash, hero map, gold tier) than the console, and the console
borrows only its precise details: the brand mark, the background wash, fine
edges and the Thailand dot map as real data.

## Colors

Primary marks actions and selection. Success, warning and danger describe
real account, payment or telemetry state. Muted text must remain legible.

- Gold marks the dedicated tier on the sales page and nothing else; it never
  simulates payment or availability state.
- Chart series use `series-1` (indigo) and `series-2` (gold-ochre): steps
  validated against the panel surface (dark band L 0.48–0.67, CVD ΔE ≈ 26).
  Status colours are never a series colour.
- The sales page uses darker raised surfaces (`sales-surface`) than the
  console panel; the console sidebar is the `sidebar` layer.

## Typography

Use the incumbent Thai-capable system stack. Reserve monospace for actual
connection data, identifiers and measurements. Keep Thai copy at 1.5 line height.
The sales display heading loosens to 1.32 in Thai and 1.25 in Japanese and
Chinese so tone marks and CJK never clip. No gradient text: emphasis comes from
size and weight.

## Layout

Admin uses its existing sidebar and responsive bottom navigation. New sales
sections join one Sales destination. The customer portal uses a compact header,
own-account navigation and a task workspace. Narrow layouts stack fields and
convert data rows to readable records; never hide essential actions off-screen.

The portal header reads brand · public discovery (plans, guide) · account
destinations (my plans, orders) · language and sign-in. Sales-page sections use
a heading rail beside the content, separated by hairlines.

The admin Overview reads business row, network row, then history (one range
control scopes every history below it), the fleet map, board tiles, the order
pipeline, and attention/activity.

## Elevation & Depth

Use tonal surfaces and one fine boundary per container. Avoid glowing shadows.
Shadows that exist carry an offset and blur (hero art, primary buttons on the
sales page only).

## Shapes

Panels retain gently curved corners. Small pills carry status only.

## Components

Reuse primary/secondary buttons, labeled inputs, usage bars, empty states and
accessible tables. Editors pause refresh. Every mutation has pending, success
and actionable failure states; payments show their actual lifecycle.

- **Readiness banner:** icon + bold state + next step; the server renders it
  for its own readiness and portal.js only replaces it when the state differs.
- **Fleet map:** `admin-api/dashboard/thailand-map.svg` (generated by
  `tools/render_landing_map.py`, projection in data attributes). Markers link
  to the device drawer; filled = saved position, dashed ring = geo-IP estimate;
  nearby boards merge into one numbered marker with the worst state.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Keep existing device, assignment, OTA and connection-sheet workflows intact.
- Show missing configuration and unavailable capacity explicitly.
- Keep customer credentials masked until deliberately revealed.
- Do not invent prices, SLA, inventory, exclusive IP claims or payment success.
- Sales-page art is illustration, labelled as such; never a live-connection claim.
